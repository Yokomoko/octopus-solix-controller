How these are worked out
  • Imported, exported and cost come from your meter for the whole day, refreshed every 10 minutes.
  • Pending is the car charging outside a slot Octopus has told us about. Octopus usually confirms it as a smart slot afterwards, so it's costed at off-peak. If it isn't confirmed within 2 hours it moves to peak.
  • Battery saved only counts time this app was running, because the meter can't see the battery. Each kWh used saves the rate at the time minus the off-peak rate it was charged at.
